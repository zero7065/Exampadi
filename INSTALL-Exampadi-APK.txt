========================================================
  INSTALL ExamPadi ON YOUR PHONE  (2 minutes)
========================================================

1. Tap the ExamPadi APK link to download it.

2. Open the downloaded file
   (from your notification bar, or Files > Downloads).

3. If your phone asks, tap "Settings" and turn ON
   "Allow from this source" (also called "Install
   unknown apps") for your browser or Files app,
   then go back and tap install again.

4. Tap "Install", then "Open".

5. If Google Play Protect shows
   "Unsafe app blocked", tap "More details" then
   "Install anyway". This is normal for any app that
   does not come from the Play Store.

6. Done! The ExamPadi icon is now on your home screen.

--------------------------------------------------------
The app is safe and digitally signed. It opens
full-screen with no browser bar and works offline
after the first open.

--------------------------------------------------------
SUPPORT (if the "Install unknown apps" option is hard
to find):

Android 8.0 and above:
  Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps
  > choose the app you downloaded with (Chrome / Files)
  > Allow.

Android 7 and below:
  Settings > Security > Unknown sources > turn ON.

Still stuck? Make sure you tapped the APK file itself
(the one ending in .apk), not the web link.
